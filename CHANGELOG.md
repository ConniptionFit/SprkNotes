# Changelog

## Unreleased
- Mic bleed, round 4 (the cause behind rounds 1 to 3): the speaker audio and the microphone audio drifted out of line by up to several hundred milliseconds every time playback paused and started again, and every time the app was busy turning speech into text. The echo canceller then compared the microphone with the wrong moment of the speakers and let them through, which is why a video showed up as Me mostly at the start of a phrase and in short phrases. Each piece of audio is now placed by when the device delivered it, speaker audio reaches the canceller before the matching microphone audio, and the canceller restarts when a device changes. In a 40 second simulated session with pauses and busy moments, the share of each phrase loud enough to reach the transcript went from 3% to 100% (most phrases over 30%) down to at most 9%, with your own speech kept. A two or three word Me line that repeats an Others line at the same moment is now dropped too. Needs the speakers-on check on the laptop (ADR-013).
- Speed and steadier audio: the microphone and speaker streams are now read on a thread of their own, every 20 ms, while speech is decoded on the session thread. Before, nothing was read while a phrase or a live caption was being decoded (often a few hundred milliseconds, longer on slow laptops), so the speaker stream's quiet stretches were measured from late reads, which was one cause of the mic bleed, and Pause or End waited for the decode. Everything else works as before: the same device lines, pause and resume drop audio from the pause, a capture crash still closes the file as interrupted. Needs a look on the laptop.
- Transcription accuracy: audio is now low-pass filtered before it drops to 16 kHz. The old converter did no filtering at 48 kHz, so sound above 8 kHz (s and t sounds, keyboard clicks, fan and codec hiss) folded back into the speech range as noise the models never saw in training, and the echo removal saw that same noise in a different place in each stream. The new filter keeps speech to 7 kHz at full level and removes what is above 8 kHz by 60 dB or more, adds about 2 ms of fixed delay, and costs a few million multiply-adds a second per stream. `sprkctl transcribe` and `bench accuracy` use the same filter for WAV files, so bench numbers match what the app hears. Needs a WER run on the laptop fixtures to measure the gain.
- Transcription accuracy: each utterance now keeps 0.3 s of audio after the point where speech detection says it ended. Detection ends speech where its confidence first drops, which is often inside the quiet tail of the last word (a final s, t or k), so the engine dropped or misheard last words. The added audio is always already heard and is silence by the detector's own measure, so it never takes words from the next utterance. Needs a bench run on the laptop fixtures to measure the gain.
- Library: folders, rename, move and delete (SPEC 8.4). The list has a folder picker (All transcripts, Top of the library, or one folder with its subfolders), **New folder** and **Rename folder**. An open transcript gains **Move** (a menu of folders), **Rename** (the title in the file and the file name both change; the date and time at the front of the name stay) and **Delete**, which asks first and moves the transcript and its sidecar to the Recycle Bin (summaries and exports next to it stay). The transcript of the session that is running can't be renamed, moved or deleted until End, and neither can the folder it is in. Folder names that would leave the library or hide a folder are refused. Drag to move is not in yet. Needs a check on Windows (Recycle Bin, Explorer view).
- Fix (Bluetooth headsets and calls): the app now uses your Windows *communications* microphone and speakers by default (where a headset's hands-free mic and call audio live), not only the console default, so a headset in a meeting is the device that gets transcribed. Settings has a Microphone and an Output list to correct it, and the transcript names the devices in use ("Microphone: ...", "Others audio: ...") so a wrong device is visible. See ADR-014 for why separate recognizers were not needed.
- Mic bleed, round 3 (alpha.4 still leaked): a video through the speakers could still show up as Me, mostly in the first seconds of a playback and in short phrases. Mic sound that is not clearly louder than the speaker bleed the app expects (learned during the session) is now muted, so quiet leakage no longer reaches the transcript. Tested with a reverberant room model; real laptop check still needed (ADR-013).
- Choose the microphone: Settings has a Microphone list (System default or a specific input; the Output list is in the entry above). An unplugged pick falls back to the default and returns when plugged back in. Switching mid-session writes a "Source changed" line.
- Fix: Summarize with Claude now delivers the transcript. Claude could not see the attached file or write to the folder, so the clipboard now holds the instructions plus the transcript text; Claude opens with a one-line start and a toast says to press Ctrl+V. The summary prompt asks for the summary in the reply instead of a saved file. Needs a check on the work laptop.
- Start and End show that they are working. Start no longer freezes the window: the model now loads off the main thread, and the button turns into a spinner reading "Loading model: <model name>" until the session begins. End shows "Saving transcript" with a spinner while the file is closed off. The overlay (mini) Start and Stop buttons show the same spinner and tooltip. Needs a look on the laptop.
- Mic bleed, round 2 (alpha.2 still leaked): AEC3 only cancels echo that arrives 50 to 450 ms after the reference, and the mic and loopback streams are not aligned that well in real use. The echo path now tracks the offset between the streams and keeps it in AEC3's range, and a backstop gate mutes mic speech that still follows the loudness pattern of the speakers. Needs the speakers-on check again on the laptop.
- Fix: Export to Claude opened the app with an empty chat box. The shell cuts links near 2,000 characters and the full prompt link was longer, so the link now carries a short prompt when needed, and the full prompt is always copied (a toast says to press Ctrl+V if the box is empty). Fix: the Summarize app menu now closes when you click away or press Esc. New Home button in the bottom bar returns to the transcript page. Needs a check on the work laptop.
- Smoother live captions (alpha): the live row no longer snaps around. A visible word is replaced in place only once its new wording has settled, and a flipped word no longer hides the words after it (they used to vanish and retype); the row shrinks only if a shorter reading persists for 0.6 s. The word being typed keeps its space reserved, so it never jumps to the next line mid-word. Words fade in and ease between soft and settled instead of popping, the live row stays in place briefly when it empties, and the transcript slides up by one line (220 ms) instead of snapping when a line is added or replaced. Reduced motion turns the animation off. Needs a look on the laptop.
- Ask Sprk is easy to find and works like a chat: a labeled **Ask Sprk** button sits in the bottom bar whenever Ask Sprk is available (it opens Settings when it is off). The panel shows the conversation top to bottom with a typed-question box at the bottom, ready-made question buttons (What did I miss?, Action items, What was decided?, What is still open?, Who owns what?) and, when it cannot answer yet, says why with an Open Settings button.
- Mic bleed fix (ADR-013, alpha): speaker sound picked up by the mic (a video playing through speakers was labelled Me) is now removed with WebRTC AEC3 using the system stream as reference, and the system stream keeps wall-clock time while silent (Windows loopback sends nothing during silence, which pushed Others lines minutes away from the matching Me lines so dedupe never matched). Needs a speakers-on check on the laptops.
- Fix (Windows): Summarize said "No Claude or Copilot app found" when the apps were installed from the Microsoft Store (MSIX). Store apps register their links through the package, not the classic `HKCR\<scheme>\shell\open\command` key the check looked at. Detection now also accepts a `URL Protocol` marker, the shell's own answer for the link (`AssocQueryString`), and the installed Store package (Claude; Microsoft 365 Copilot app). The Copilot link falls back to `ms-copilot:` when `ms-officeapp:` has no handler. Linux detection is unchanged. Needs a check on the work laptop.
- Audio source lines: transcripts no longer get `Audio source started/ended` lines (pilot feedback: noise, and the title was wrong). The title was the focused window's, so it flipped as the user changed tabs; a browser's window title is no longer used at all, and other apps use it only when they have a single titled window. Old transcripts still read as before.
- Ask Sprk model download (ADR-010 addendum): Settings > Ask Sprk shows the model state with a Download model button (per size), progress, and Check for updates. The Ask Sprk switch stays disabled until a model is installed. The Ask panel has a text box for typed questions and three ready-made question chips (what was decided, what is still open, who owns what). Index entries for assistant packs (`kind`, `tier`), pack size limit raised to 4 GiB, `scripts/publish-assistant-model.sh` to publish packs.
- Portable distribution source: the repository address the app updates and downloads models from now lives only in `distribution.toml` (compiled in; `SPRK_REPO_URL` and `SPRK_REPO_PROVIDER` override at build time; policy and MSI switches still override at run time). A GitHub copy of the CI workflow (`.github/workflows/ci.yml`) with a parity check, and `docs/forking.md`: a checklist for moving to another repository (keep the MSI `UpgradeCode`) or forking a separately branded edition (new `UpgradeCode` and identifier).
- Release process (ADR-012, `docs/releasing.md`): alpha, beta and stable from SemVer tags with a short checklist. A "Cut release" workflow (or `scripts/cut-release.sh`) picks the next number, bumps the version and Cargo.lock, commits and tags; the Release workflow checks the tag, re-runs the tests and creates a draft release with notes made from the CHANGELOG. A promotion is the tested alpha plus a version-only commit, checked in CI (`scripts/release-version.sh guard`). `scripts/publish-release.sh` uploads the Windows files one per request with a scoped token, checks sizes, and refuses to publish without the update manifest; `yank` turns a bad release back into a draft. `scripts/winvm.sh release-tag` builds an exact tag and `dist` copies the result back. `bash scripts/ship-alpha.sh` does cut, Windows build, upload and publish in one go on the RX9070 (works from fish).
- Fix: alpha and beta builds (for example `0.2.0-alpha.1`) refused every model pack as "app too old", because the pack compatibility check only understood plain `X.Y.Z` versions. A pre-release now counts as its release number. Found by CI on the first alpha tag; `v0.2.0-alpha.1` is skipped, use the next alpha.

## 0.1.0
- Enterprise install and app updates (ADR-011): one-line per-user install (`install.ps1`), update checks against a Forgejo or GitHub release source, channels (stable, beta, alpha) chosen in Settings, a signed `update-manifest.json` per release (SSHSIG, namespace `sprknotes-update`), and an Updates panel in Settings. Admin controls: policy keys `UpdateMode`, `UpdateChannel`, `UpdateRepoUrl`, `UpdateProvider`, `UpdatePublicKeys`, `UpdateCheckHours` (a key set by policy locks its control), MSI switches (`UPDATE_CHANNEL`, `UPDATE_REPO_URL`, `UPDATE_MODE`, `UPDATE_PUBLIC_KEY`, `LOCK_UPDATES`, `UPDATE_TASK`), installer defaults that never lock, and a SYSTEM scheduled task (`SprkNotes\Update`) that keeps per-machine installs current without a window. `release.ps1` writes and signs the update manifest and accepts `-beta.N` / `-alpha.N` versions. New crate `sprk-update`; ADMX draft in `packaging/admx`; research in `docs/enterprise-install-research.md`. Needs a real-machine check (see manual checks).
- Ask Sprk, in the app (ADR-009; off by default, all extras off until chosen): Settings section (on/off, size, a switch per feature), an Ask button and panel (What did I miss?, Action items, a question box, with timestamps for the lines each answer came from), and a "Suggest a title" button for an unnamed session. Optional extras, each its own switch: agenda check, my commitments, pin a moment, parking lot, explain this, say it again short, next-meeting prep. New policy key `AssistantFeatures` (allow-list). Everything runs on this PC; question history is in memory only. The release script now builds and ships the `sprk-assistant` helper for both architectures. New Gemma 4 prompt format.
- Assistant features, engine side (ADR-009, not in the UI yet): cited recap of the last N minutes (piece by piece for long windows, then merged), action items with owner and due only when stated (deduplicated across pieces), and a question answerer that finds the relevant lines with BM25 search and answers from them or says "not stated". Every claim is checked in plain code: citations must be lines the model was shown, and numbers, weekdays, months and names in a claim must appear in the cited lines or the participant list; anything that fails is dropped instead of shown.
- Assistant tooling: `sprkctl model ... --kind assistant` installs and lists assistant packs in their own folder (`assistant-models`); a transcription store refuses an assistant pack with a plain reason. `sprk-assistant-bench` (load time, prompt speed, time to first text, generation speed, peak memory, optional background load) for the laptop benchmark; `scripts/make-assistant-pack-folder.py` turns a GGUF into a pack folder.
- Assistant engine (ADR-009, still off and not wired to the UI): the llama.cpp helper (`crates/sprk-assistant-llama`, CPU, built on its own because it compiles C++) behind the same pipe protocol, with shared stream helpers (whole UTF-8 characters, stop strings across pieces) and `effective` / `status` logic that merges policy with the user's choice (off by default, Light default, policy locks). Tested through the real binary against a tiny random-weight model made by `scripts/make-test-assistant-model.py` (no download); real models and real timings still need the laptops.
- Assistant helper process (ADR-009): the `sprk-assistant` helper binary and `RemoteEngine`, the app-side client that runs it at below-normal priority and speaks the pipe protocol. Hold, resume and cancel follow the caller's run control while a request runs; a dead helper is a plain failure, never a hang. No real engine is compiled in yet, so Load answers `load_failed` until the llama.cpp adapter lands.
- Assistant foundation (ADR-009, ADR-010; off by default, nothing visible yet): new `sprk-assistant` crate (engine trait with a scripted fake, line-numbered transcript view, app-owned prompt formats, dynamic note titles with cleaning and a grounding check, the resource governor for tier choice, lag yield, idle unload and thread caps, the helper pipe protocol); `model.json` gains an optional `kind` (`transcription` default, or `assistant`) and an `assistant` section, and a store accepts one kind; policy keys `AssistantEnabled`, `AssistantTier`, `AssistantPinnedModel`, `AssistantIdleUnloadMinutes`, `AssistantMaxThreads`, `AssistantSaveOutputs`, `AssistantBackground`. No model runs yet: the llama.cpp helper and the laptop benchmark come next.
- Open items from the overnight sweep: speaker-bleed removal also catches a mic line that is a piece of a longer remote line or spans two (containment test, 4 words or more, 80% in order, in addition to whole-line similarity); Linux default input and output switches are now noticed (the device name was constant), so a Source changed line is written; resume restarts the capture streams so audio buffered during the pause is not stamped as new (Others lines were about 2 s late); live captions lower a title-cased run of three or more words so they match the saved line; Start falls back to ~/Documents when no Documents folder is configured.
